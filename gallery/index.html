字隙微光

SDXL Lightning

A wide typographic specimen on obsidian-black card, arranged in two balanced panels inside a fine Art Deco frame. Build a single ivory glyph from geometric strokes over a champagne-gold calligraphy grid on the left, then use measured ivory rules and a small velvet-crimson seal on the right. Graze the panels from the upper left so foil edges glint and the pressed paper fibers stay visible in the shadows.
